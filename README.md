## 📥 How to Obtain Tokens

Tokens are initially held by the deploying wallet (`0x2ed855874404938C3299d8Dc3627B5FE7244EFAA`). To request test tokens:
1. Connect your Web3 wallet (e.g., MetaMask) on the Sepolia Testnet to the [Live Dashboard](https://amandabramberga.github.io/token-dashboard/).
2. Click the **"Add MTK to Wallet"** or **"Add SLV13 to Wallet"** buttons to import the custom token contract directly into MetaMask.
3. Contact the deployer wallet owner with your public address to receive test allocation transfers.
